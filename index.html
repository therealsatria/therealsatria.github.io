About

Professional summary

I am a software developer and IT infrastructure professional based in Lamongan, East Java, Indonesia. My background combines backend development with hands-on system administration, which means I understand both the application layer and the environment it runs on. I have worked as a Technical Consultant, a System Administrator, and a backend developer — often as the person clients and colleagues turn to when something breaks and the cause is not obvious.

My strongest skill is troubleshooting and root cause analysis. I prefer to find out why an issue happened rather than apply a temporary fix, and I document the result so it does not come back. I work carefully, communicate clearly, and take ownership of the systems I am responsible for — including a period as the sole IT person for a hospital environment, where reliability was not optional.

I am open to international opportunities, including remote roles and relocation to the GCC region (Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, UAE), Japan, Malaysia, Singapore, and other markets. I adapt quickly to new environments, tools, and working cultures.
